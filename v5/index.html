Suning (Simon) Liu

I am an educator living in Auckland, New Zealand.

Before moving here I spent over six years (September 2018 to February 2025) at Zhejiang Vocational Academy of Art in Hangzhou, working in student support, careers guidance and programme coordination. I also taught blind and visually impaired learners at Zhejiang Special Education Vocational College. These days I am interested in how generative AI can help with learning and career planning, and I build small tools to try ideas out, such as kiwileap. My Master of Education (Research) thesis at the University of Auckland has been accepted; the degree is not yet conferred.
