About

I worked at Zhejiang Vocational Academy of Art, a public arts college in Hangzhou, from September 2018 to February 2025. I held three roles there: Student Engagement Officer, then Counsellor in the School of Drama and Film, then Student Programmes Coordinator. Most of the work was looking after students: pastoral care, wellbeing, careers support and the programmes that run around their studies.

In 2021 I was seconded to Zhejiang Special Education Vocational College, where I taught blind and visually impaired learners in a piano tuning programme. Adapting materials for each learner taught me a lot about how teaching has to fit the person.

As a counsellor I designed and piloted conversational generative-AI tools for career planning. They were organised around the stages of career planning and helped students name their strengths, clarify their interests and map out possible paths. Since then I have kept building small tools, which are listed on the Projects page.

I studied for a Master of Education (Research) at the University of Auckland. My thesis is a systematic review of generative AI in Chinese higher vocational education. It has been accepted, and the degree is not yet conferred. Before that I completed a Bachelor of Arts in Musicology (Arts Management) at the Central Conservatory of Music while working full-time.

In Auckland I volunteer as a cross-cultural conversation facilitator with Acquaint NZ and with the Motutapu Island Restoration Trust. I speak Mandarin and English.
