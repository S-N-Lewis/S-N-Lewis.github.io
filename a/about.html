About

I spent over six years supporting students at a 6,000-student arts college in Hangzhou, moving through three roles to a senior coordination post. Now in Auckland, I build and test AI tools that help people learn and work.

I am an educator from Hangzhou, now based in Auckland. From 2018 to 2025 I worked at Zhejiang Vocational Academy of Art, a public arts college with more than 6,000 students. I started as a student engagement officer, became a counsellor in the School of Drama and Film, and finished as senior coordinator of student programmes across seven faculties.

While working, I started using generative AI with students: I designed and piloted conversational tools that walk a student through each stage of career planning. That led to my master's at the University of Auckland, where my thesis is a PRISMA 2020 systematic review of generative AI in Chinese higher vocational education.

What transfers from all this: I learn new tools fast, test them against real users, and turn them into workflows other people can rely on.

The question behind my work

My research asks what happens to a student's judgement of their own work once a machine has touched it.
